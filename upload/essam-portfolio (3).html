Odin

Designer & builder · third iteration · 2024 → now

AI Automation · Multi-Agent

Odin is my AI automation assistant — a small company of five specialised sub-agents that I direct. Each agent has one job and does it well. Everything they produce passes through a human approval gate: me. Nothing ships that I haven't read, checked and signed off.

Vanguard Clash

Solo developer · design, code, art pipeline

Browser Game · Procedural

A hero shooter that runs entirely in the browser — no installs, no engine. Every map, weapon and visual effect is procedurally generated, so no two matches ever play the same twice.

  • Fully procedural maps, weapons and effects
  • Hero roster with distinct kits and roles
  • Runs in-browser on vanilla JavaScript + Canvas
  • Bot matches with scaling difficulty
